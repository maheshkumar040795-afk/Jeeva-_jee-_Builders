How We Work

From First Call to Handover Keys

A clear 8-step journey from your first idea to a finished space.

01

Consultation

We understand your requirements and budget, and discuss options.

02

Design & Estimate

Layout, material selection and an itemised cost estimate for your approval.

03

Material Selection

Steel, cement, tiles and fittings finalised and sourced from trusted brands.

04

Foundation & Structure

Excavation, footing and RCC structural work carried out to engineering standards.

05

Wall Work & Roofing

Brickwork, plastering and roof slab completed with regular quality checks.

06

Electrical & Plumbing

Wiring, piping and fittings installed in coordination with the finishing plan.

07

Finishing & Interiors

Flooring, painting doors, windows and interior finishing brought together.

08

Handover

Final walkthrough, snag fixes, and the keys handed over — on schedule.
